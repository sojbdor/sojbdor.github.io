Clair-Obscur

After the hypnotic journey of “Turning Point” and her explosive collaboration with Marco Simioni, Sukkube compose five modular tracks. This album is the result of several years of research, notably involving a reworking of her latest EP, ‘Turning Point’. She has reworked the melodies to improve them and create a dialogue between them.
Firmly determined to steer clear of computer-based composition. She wrote and composed all the tracks during a week-long studio residency at Microgroove in Toulouse, with the help of André Louis Jouan (audio director), Masami Komuro (recording engineer) and Éric Anglas (performance coach).
These are recorded and edited clips of a live session at Microgroove studio during the night called "Sound Lab"; this EP and live have been performed at Metronum, Les Inouïs du Printemps de Bourges, About Blank (Berlin). We did a very limited t-shirt and tote bag with Julia Castel and Arman handcrafted edition in serigraphy that you can purchase on bandcamp.

Production: Super Brut & Inouïs du Printemps de Bourges
Label: Detroit Underground
Sound: Sukkube/Charlène Levasseur
Designer: Julia Castel
Audio Director/Mix: Andre Louis Jouan
Audio Recording: Masami Komuro
Video Editing: Jeremy Holveck
Videographers: Beli Bldet and Kaliski prod
Mastering: Andre Louis Jouan
Released July 10, 2026

Dark Triggers

They met at Gaswerk for a residency of 1 week, in October 2021. They both started in sound design on modular systems. The "Dark Triggers" EP has been created through 3 big jam sessions where the main patterns were found. During live sessions Sukkube works with a eurorack system and her VCOs are most of the time used without a sequencer and envelopes which results in a huge layer of metallic noises without boundaries, while also utilising a TR-09 for the main rhythm. Sage-S set is full eurorack and he has a powerful sequencer to design an accurate sound and groovy patterns

The River Of Neïd

Neïd is a city built on ancient ruins. Situated from miles and miles underground, the river of Neïd flows around the tower of the rabbit master to throw itself into the black sea of the North West of the city. Sea And Bunkers

Eccentric soundscapes, cryptic atmospheres, unexpected rhythms - with the second episode of the Intelliance series, the concept label Augmented Research once again holds up a mirror to the progressive present and provides various perspectives on the innovative electronic club sound of the next generation.
With "Sea And Bunkers", Sukkube proves her virtuoso and versatile handling of modular sound synthesis. The rapid, loopy beat, accompanied by a serious and simultaneously playful melody and hissing atmospheres, generates a mysterious, fresh mood. Influences from different eras form an interesting symbiosis of futuristic, uplifting techno and nuances of classic styles.

Written by Sukkube
Mix: Sukkube
Master: Sawlin at Precision Mastering, Berlin
Label: Augmented Research
Artwork: Anton Braun
Premiere: BCCO
Released May 20, 2024
