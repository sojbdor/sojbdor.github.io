Spinning Groove

Citizen Charlène Levasseur returns to the Fallen Metropolis with entry number 142 in the catalogue - "Sukkube - Spinning Groove". Six raw tracks by Sukkube, recorded live using analog modular synthesizers, a Roland-09 hybrid, and a Allen Heath ZED-16. The sessions took place at Théâtre Le Phare in Toulouse, during a Nanajam dance event, where sound met movement and both created an intimate dialogue.

Written and mixed by Sukkube at IPN Studio, Toulouse
Label: Fallen Metropolis
Released April 1, 2024
