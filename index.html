SUKKUBE what is it?
Live act, djsets and electronic music productions based in Toulouse, France. She creates dystopian, non standard, whispered techno. Her style is inspired by artists such as Laurent Garnier, Ben Klock and Andy Stott.

At the age of six, her grandmother gave her a synthesiser. That was when her musical journey began; her mother used to listen to Depeche Mode and Amy Winehouse. She learnt to play the guitar with her neighbour and formed her first emo rock band. Later, she met Wizard (aka Clémentine Méreau), who showed her a few tricks on Ableton.

In 2016, she discovered Dommune radio and stumbled across DJ sets by the likes of Bruna and Stanislav Tolkachev, as well as tracks by Porter Ricks, Positive Centre, DJ Rush, Roma Zuckerman and Peter Van Hoesen.

After graduating from isdaT (Institute Superior of the Arts of Toulouse), she moved to Berlin, where she has been performing live since 2021. The same year, she founded Associate Records.

Since 2023, she has been collaborating with Force Inc. Mille Plateaux, presenting modular performances. In 2024, she joined the IPN collective in Toulouse, where she coordinates artistic events and concerts.

Renowned for her live sound performances, she has performed at: About Blank (Berlin), Acud Macht Neue (Berlin), Superbooth (Berlin), gART.n (Berlin), Fitzroy (Berlin), HFG Kapelle (Frankfurt Am Main), Forum Klub (Graz), Ved Siden Af (Copenhagen), Le Groove (Geneva), Metronum (Toulouse), Le Printemps de Bourges (Bourges), LaPéniche (Chalon-sur-Saône).
